How RideLink Fair-Share Cost Splitting Works (in ₹)

Under campus guidelines, carpool contributions are non-commercial reimbursements calculated strictly from fuel expenses. The formula used across all Bhopal rides is:

Per-Passenger Contribution (₹) = Total Trip Fuel Expense ÷ (Offered Passenger Seats + 1 Driver)

Example: A ₹120 commute from MANIT to RKMP offering 3 passenger seats splits 4 ways among 4 travelers (₹120 ÷ 4) = ₹30 per person (estimated). The driver covers their own equal share.
